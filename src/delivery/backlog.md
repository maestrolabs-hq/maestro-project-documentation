# Backlog model

The [repository backlog contract](https://github.com/maestrolabs-hq/maestro-project-documentation/blob/main/BACKLOG.md)
defines readiness. This page explains the model without creating another list.

## Entry means ready

A backlog item has already passed human approval. It names an owning
repository, an observable outcome, functional and non-functional requirements,
acceptance criteria, a definition of done, an evidence plan, and dependencies.

An item missing one of those fields remains a proposal. Raw ideas do not enter
the backlog, and producing more documents does not promote them automatically.

## Scope follows ownership

An initiative may cross repositories. Each epic has one owner and advances a
slice that can be accepted independently. Tasks are derived only after that
slice is ready for development.

## Views are not new sources

The organization portfolio is the operational priority view. The
[roadmap](roadmap.md) is its public projection. Neither this page nor the
roadmap duplicates a mutable queue.
