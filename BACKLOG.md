# Backlog

The delivery contract for approved Maestro initiatives and repository-owned
epics. This file defines readiness; it is not a list of raw ideas.

## Entry criteria

An item enters the backlog only when all of the following are explicit:

- **Approval:** the proposal has passed its human decision gate.
- **Owner:** one repository owns delivery, even when other repositories depend
  on the result.
- **Outcome:** the observable change and the reader or operator who benefits.
- **Functional requirements:** behavior the result must provide.
- **Non-functional requirements:** constraints such as security, reliability,
  portability, accessibility, and performance.
- **Acceptance criteria:** checks that distinguish completion from progress.
- **Definition of done:** implementation, tests, documentation, review, and
  release obligations.
- **Evidence plan:** where proof will be produced and how it will be checked.
- **Dependencies:** preceding work and external conditions that can block
  delivery.

An item missing any entry criterion remains a proposal. Promotion is a human
approval event, not an automatic consequence of producing documents.

## Boundaries

Ideas are captured before the backlog. Tasks are derived after an initiative or
epic is accepted. Repository TODO files remain for trivial local cleanup and do
not substitute for this readiness contract.

The organization portfolio is the prioritized operational view. The public
roadmap is a projection of approved direction, not another mutable backlog.
