# Context

Glossary for the Maestro estate view. Terms only, no implementation detail.

## Idea

An uncommitted thought. An idea may be captured without implying priority,
feasibility, or intent to build it.

## Proposal

An idea undergoing discovery. Its purpose, requirements, risks, and evidence
are being challenged before a commitment is made.

## Initiative

An approved Maestro-level outcome. It may cross repository boundaries and is
decomposed only after its outcome and evidence are clear.

## Epic

A coherent delivery slice assigned to one owning repository. An epic advances
an initiative and has acceptance criteria that can be verified independently.

## Task

A development-ready unit of work small enough to implement, test, review, and
accept as one change.

## Backlog

The prioritized set of approved initiatives and epics that satisfy their
readiness definition. Raw ideas and incomplete proposals are not backlog
items.

## Roadmap

The public projection of approved direction. It communicates actual and future
capabilities without becoming a second task tracker.

## Claim

A factual assertion explicitly marked for machine verification. A claim names
its status, immutable source revision, and verification date.

## Evidence

The source that makes a claim checkable. Evidence is immutable and belongs
beside the implementation or decision it demonstrates.
